# Avatar asset notices

The body, head, eyes, hair and clothing in the current `pixel-layers-v3` pilot come from the
**Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator**:

- Source: https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator
- Licenses: mixed per asset (CC0, CC-BY 4.0, CC-BY-SA 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0, GPL 3.0)
- Local source, selected definitions, full credit index and license:
  `public/avatar-parts/vendor/universal-lpc/`

The project selects aligned front-facing walk frames, applies deterministic color ramps to
recolorable layers and scales them with nearest-neighbour sampling. It does not redraw the LPC
character parts. The four developer-themed ground items remain project-generated artwork
documented in `public/avatar-parts/v3/sources/README.md`.
